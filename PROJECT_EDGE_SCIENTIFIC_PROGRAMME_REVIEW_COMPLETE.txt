## 4. Research family review: opening-gap magnitude and post-open movement

### 4.1 Scientific sequence

The initial Level-1 study observed a broad pooled association between continuous
raw gap magnitude and same-day movement opposing the gap direction. It also
identified a fundamental measurement concern: the raw gap and open-to-close
outcome reused the same observed opening price with opposite algebraic signs.
No observed relationship passed every predeclared candidacy criterion.

`EDGE-L1-002` removed the opening price from the primary outcome by measuring
post-09:35 movement from an exact 09:34 close landmark. Under its recorded
executor and decision rules:

- the gap-down branch failed its primary, materiality, and persistence gates;
- the gap-up branch passed the frozen gates; and
- shared-opening-price reuse could no longer be the sole explanation of the
  surviving gap-up association.

`EDGE-L1-003` then excluded all exact effective-session/ticker keys mapped from
the frozen MDM split ledger. The gap-up result remained materially negative,
negative in every frozen era, and unlike the within-date placebo. Recorded
split-key matches therefore could not be the sole explanation.

`EDGE-L1-004` tested the fundamental remaining question: within the same
retained uppercase ticker-history proxy and frozen era, did larger gap-ups still
have materially more direction-opposed post-09:35 movement?

The frozen primary gate failed:

| Metric | Equal-date point | Equal-date 97.5% upper | Date gate | Equal-identity point | Equal-identity 97.5% upper | Identity gate | Overall |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Sign score | +0.00733 | +0.01920 | Fail | -0.02906 | -0.01491 | Fail | Fail |
| Oriented contrast | -6.725 bp | -1.273 bp | Fail | -31.442 bp | -14.497 bp | Pass | Fail |

Survival required the registered weighting and metric gates, not selection of
the more favourable estimate after inspection. The later era and placebo gates
were correctly not run under the sequential stop rule. The immutable decision
is `NOT VALIDATED - ELIMINATE`, `EDGE WEAKENED`, and
`gap_up_branch_remains_open = false`.

The first V1 execution of `EDGE-L1-004` encountered a report-rendering failure
after final lineage verification. Its retained technical record states that no
outcome values were inspected. The V2 correction changed only the freeze path,
empty-table rendering guard, and matching self-test. Reversing those changes
reconstructed the V1 executor hash exactly; scientific logic and protocol were
unchanged. The V2 scientific decision is therefore the applicable record.

Auditability is not identical across the sequence. `EDGE-L1-002` retains its
executor, exhaustive manifest, run record, and outputs, but its manifest does
not contain a separately retained `FROZEN_PROTOCOL.json` or
`PRE_OUTCOME_FREEZE.json`. `EDGE-L1-003` and `EDGE-L1-004` have those standalone
freeze artefacts. This weakens independent auditability of L1-002 relative to
the later studies, but it does not reopen the final L1-004 elimination.

## 7. Single highest-value next experiment

### 7.1 Recommendation

> **Authorise one Level-1 terminal falsification of next-session daily
> observed-range recurrence, using a new Level-1 identifier and a fresh MDM
> reconstruction.**

This is not `EDGE-VSP-E02`, confirmation, validation, or Knowledge Base
evidence. It is a separate screening study whose only purpose is to decide
whether the formal range family merits further investment.

### 7.2 Precise scientific question

Within one frozen observable MDM daily ticker-date census, is
`R(i,t) = log(high(i,t) / low(i,t))` materially positively associated with
`R(i,t+1)` after retained exact-case ticker-history effects and common
formation-date effects are removed?

The study shall have:

- the exact-case raw gzip MDM layer already found fit for ticker-key integrity,
  unless a later MDM version is audited before outcomes and proves the Parquet
  defects repaired;
- one complete observable population without scanner, price, volume, market-cap,
  sector, or gap filters;
- one operational exact-case ticker-history proxy, explicitly not certified
  security identity;
- one archive-adjacency rule and one next-session horizon;
- one discovery interval ending no later than the already exposed 2026-07-30
  boundary, with no pair allowed to cross it and all later dates untouched;
- one atomic same-row feature and the same feature as the later outcome;
- one two-way primary estimand controlling ticker-history and formation date;
- one prospectively ratified material-effect boundary and interval decision;
- date- and identity-aware dependence treatment;
- frozen influence, missingness, tie, and defect-dominance stops; and
- no alternative threshold, transform, horizon, subgroup, regime, estimator,
  feature combination, or post-result rescue.

Every exposure must remain in next-session denominator accounting even when the
later ticker row is absent; complete-case deletion cannot be silent. The charter
must freeze the missingness sensitivity or inferability rule.

All dates through 2026-07-30 are already Level-1 exposed by prior Project EDGE
work and remain so permanently. Later dates must not be read, inventoried at
outcome level, summarised, or used for diagnostics. The study must deposit an
exposed-date ledger. No previous experiment panel, estimate, or outcome may be
used to construct or tune the study.

### 7.3 Mandatory new-rule answer

**If the result is supported:**

The programme-screening uncertainty over whether the available raw MDM daily
census contains a material recurrence beyond retained-ticker-history and
common-date composition disappears. Formal H02 becomes worth considering after
its mandatory gates. The Level-1 result remains permanently non-evidence and
cannot be called a phenomenon, validation, causality, profitability, or edge.

**If the result is a precise null or material contradiction:**

The programme-screening uncertainty over whether the exact raw-census branch
has sufficient support to justify formal research disappears. That branch
terminates. Further formal investment is governance-retired as **untested**,
not scientifically falsified, unless genuinely new evidence and formal review
justify it. No parameter, horizon, regime, subgroup, estimator, or related
range-feature rescue is permitted within the Level-1 ancestry.

**If the result is inconclusive, not inferable, or invalid:**

No market or programme-screening uncertainty disappears. The branch closes
unresolved or invalid and waits for genuinely new information or an authorised
integrity repair. It does not search for a more favourable specification. This
is why adequate prospective precision, data-quality bounds, and dependency
checks are conditions of authorisation.

### 7.4 Why this experiment has the greatest outcome-independent value

The Level-1 range screen provides the greatest increase in scientific knowledge
per unit effort because:

- it tests the fundamental assumption before mechanisms, horizons, or
  confirmation are studied;
- a valid negative result closes the exact raw-census branch before formal
  infrastructure is built and may governance-retire formal investment while
  leaving formal H02 untested;
