# PROJECT_EDGE_CANONICAL_EQUITY_RESEARCH_FOUNDATION_V1

Status: **designated** on 15 August 2026 as a metadata promotion of existing validated neutral infrastructure.

This designation does not rebuild data, rerun Wave‑2, reopen any accepted Wave‑2 finding, or create a new empirical release or approval gate. The machine-readable authority is `PROJECT_EDGE_CANONICAL_EQUITY_RESEARCH_FOUNDATION_V1.json`.

## Default rule

Compatible US-equity research shall inherit the exact roots named in the machine-readable identity by default and shall validate only new inputs, fields, semantics, clocks, population or anchor extensions, transformations, and estimands introduced by the successor.

No MDM, PIT, survivorship, listing/security-episode, corporate-action, calendar, or full-history re-audit or rebuild is required absent one of the following:

- a concrete hash or integrity failure;
- a documented failure mechanism affecting the inherited estimand;
- an upstream source revision; or
- a genuinely new data requirement.

A changed scientific hypothesis alone does not invalidate the foundation.

Inheritance is scope-preserving, not a claim of universal equivalence. A materialized sidecar may be reused only when its grain, date range, population, clock, identity/action rules, anchor, and horizon support the successor estimand. Otherwise, construct the smallest additive delta from the retained canonical history or price spine; do not rebuild the foundation.

The designation makes this infrastructure reusable. It does not make any Wave‑1 or Wave‑2 predictor, threshold, cell, or result a universal feature definition.

## Exposed developmental ancestry

Wave‑1 and Wave‑2 are exposed E1 developmental ancestry, not E3 confirmation. Wave‑2 is accepted as the completed E1 daily breakout-event result and shall not be reopened or rerun for engineering, architectural, numerical, documentation, or performance refinement.

The carry-forward interpretation is:

> A generic daily breakout event is not, by itself, a sufficient positive-expectancy filter.

The exposed observations retained in the JSON identity include the separation of structural leadership from extension/exhaustion; the failure of generic duration, contraction, and tightness as standalone edges; the negative role of deep deterioration; ordinary-expectancy weakness of generic level/expansion breakouts; expansion's simultaneous upper-tail gain and body deterioration; early favourable excursion among successful paths; the distinction between formal breakout failure and ordinary adverse excursion; and the small descriptive increment from daily volume.

Future exploratory work must carry these observations forward, may challenge them with new evidence, and may not present them as E3-confirmed facts.

## Deliberate limitations

- Dense paths cover the frozen scoped population, not all 7.45 million Wave‑2 event rows.
- Endpoint outcomes use the Wave‑2 anchor. A new entry anchor requires the smallest additive outcome delta from the retained price spine.
- SPY, QQQ, and IWM rows exist in canonical daily OHLCV; benchmark continuity checks and regime transformations remain Wave‑3 deltas.
- PIT peer classifications are not promoted by this identity and require a bounded delta admission.
- Intraday data and its timestamp semantics are not admitted.
- There is no claim about total returns/dividends, costs, execution, capacity, sizing, strategy validity, or scanner grades.
- There is no E3 untouched-confirmation claim and no successor-period stitch.
- The B–M shared-causal-layer package remains a static/synthetic design contract, not a production canonical release.

## Successor use

Every successor should bind this foundation identity, list the exact inherited roots it actually consumes, and provide a short delta manifest. Validation effort belongs on that delta. Foundation-wide work is out of scope unless a listed invalidator is documented.
