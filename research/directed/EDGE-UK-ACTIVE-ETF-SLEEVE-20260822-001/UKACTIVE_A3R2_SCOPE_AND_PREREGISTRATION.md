# UKACTIVE-A3R2 scope and preregistration

Declared before portfolio results on 2026-08-23 from Git checkpoint `c30de89d0ceee74ea024708e7c95270bae38d150` and tag `ukactive-a3r2-preflight-20260823`.

A3R2 asks whether the corrected A3R1R1 rotation candidates support a contemporary, research-only portfolio process. The primary decision window is the final five calendar years through the final valid A2R2 XLON session; the final three years confirm, and full/pre-2020 history provides context. A3R1R1's negative geography and sector conclusions are retained. Geography, country and sector remain queryable market intelligence regardless of portfolio efficacy.

The portfolio screen is restricted to Industry, Theme, and their deduplicated union; seven signals, five selection rules and three fixed review schedules are declared in `config/UKACTIVE_A3R2_POLICY_v1.json`. Equal weighting isolates signal/count/cadence in A3R2S. Weighting research is gated and, if authorised, is limited to five declared methods with a 50% multi-position cap.

Signals form after a review close. Execution is the first valid close on or after the next XLON session, within three sessions; new weights first earn the following session's return. Prices and returns are never forward-filled. Missing, stale, proxy and pre-inception values remain invalid.

The two short-horizon hypotheses are explicitly post-A3R1R1 additions: `SHORT_RS_1_2_3_EQ` and `SHORT_RS_1_2_3_PERSISTENT`. They are labelled `A3R2_PREDECLARED_NEW_HYPOTHESIS`, not untouched confirmation.

Current Interactive Investor observations supplied by the user are current-only metadata dated 2026-08-23. Historical retail, ISA/SIPP and broker eligibility remain unresolved and will not be back-projected.

No A3R2 result is a deployment recommendation. A3R2 does not introduce cash filters, stop losses, hysteresis, macro forecasts, machine learning, overlap caps or tax optimisation.
